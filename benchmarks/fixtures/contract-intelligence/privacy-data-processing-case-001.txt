The controller shall notify the supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach, unless the personal data breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

Where the personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the controller shall communicate the personal data breach to the data subject without undue delay.

The processor must not process personal data except on documented instructions from the controller.
