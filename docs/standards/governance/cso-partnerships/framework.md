# Governed CSO Partnership Framework (2026)

## Core operating logic
1. Map the civic ecosystem continuously, including civic-space, digital, and safety conditions.
2. Separate identification from selection.
3. Choose the instrument that matches the real relationship.
4. Assess partner capacity across mission, delivery, finance, safeguarding, data, and integrity.
5. Apply threshold-based controls and corrective plans.
6. Use advisory structures with recusal and feedback loops.
7. Maintain an evidence pack across signature, implementation, and closeout.
8. Treat closeout as ecosystem strengthening, not only transaction completion.

## Instrument choice
- **Implementing partner** — delegated management of outputs and funds where partner capacity supports it.
- **Procurement** — bounded service or goods delivery where programme control remains internal.
- **Grant** — catalytic support for locally generated initiative space.
- **LTA / roster** — repeated or rapid engagement conditions.
- **Strategic partnership / MOU** — collaboration or convening relationship that is not disguised procurement.

## Minimum control gates
- No higher-risk engagement without minimum safeguarding controls.
- No delegated fund management without minimum financial controls.
- No sensitive-data handling without a data schedule and baseline security controls.
- No advisory mechanism without conflict disclosure and recusal rules.

## Minimum evidence pack
- Partner profile
- Instrument decision worksheet
- Capacity assessment
- Threshold / assessment record
- Safeguarding and incident-channel record
- Data governance schedule
- Reporting calendar and corrective-action log
- Closeout note and lessons capture
