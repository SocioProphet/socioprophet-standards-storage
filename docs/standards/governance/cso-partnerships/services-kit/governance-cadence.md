# Governance Cadence

## Standing rhythm
- Weekly operating check:
- Biweekly or monthly governance review:
- Quarterly partner and evidence review:
- Incident-triggered emergency review path:

## Core participants
- Delivery owner:
- Governance owner:
- Partner lead:
- Safeguarding or risk focal point:
- Data or reporting lead:

## Standard agenda
1. Progress against scope and outcomes
2. New or changed risks
3. Evidence-pack completeness
4. Incident review or near misses
5. Corrective actions and owners
6. Approval decisions or escalations

## Records to maintain
- Meeting note or decision log
- Updated corrective-action list
- Current risk status
- Evidence-pack delta
