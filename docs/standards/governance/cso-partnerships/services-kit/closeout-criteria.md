# Closeout Criteria

## Preconditions for closeout
- Scope delivered or formally rebaselined
- Required evidence pack complete
- Open corrective actions resolved or explicitly transferred
- Incident status resolved or handed off with owner and timeline
- Data disposition or handback recorded

## Closeout packet
- Final decision note
- Final evidence-pack index
- Outstanding risks note
- Lessons learned summary
- Handover or archive location

## Closure questions
- Did the selected instrument remain appropriate?
- Did reporting cadence match the risk profile?
- What controls were most burdensome or insufficient?
- What should change before the next engagement?
