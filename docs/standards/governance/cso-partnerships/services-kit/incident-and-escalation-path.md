# Incident and Escalation Path

## Intake routes
- Service delivery channel:
- Governance channel:
- Safeguarding channel:
- Data or security channel:

## First-hour actions
1. Stabilize immediate risk to people or data
2. Record the basic incident facts
3. Assign an escalation owner
4. Decide whether delivery must pause, narrow, or continue with controls

## Escalation triggers
- Safeguarding or misconduct concern
- Sensitive-data exposure or loss
- Civic-space retaliation or partner-targeting risk
- Fraud or material control failure
- Material divergence from approved scope

## Minimum record
- Incident ID
- Time reported
- Category
- Immediate action taken
- Owner
- Next review deadline
